Wildfire mitigation milestones — 10–20 ft High Exposure pathway

Organized to match the IBHS 10–20 ft High Exposure pathway: Ember Defense → Spread Defense → Structure Defense → Neighborhood Resilience. The milestone is the outcome; the pathway determines the task sequence. Milestones 1 and 2 are Essential; milestones 3 and 4 are Enhanced — protection to add once the essentials are in place.

Circle size is how well a measure performs for each of the four attributes: effectiveness at reducing fire risk, cost, effects on livability or quality of life, and maintenance needs. Circle color is how confident we are in that score, kept separate on purpose: a measure can look good on a dimension where our basis for saying so is thin.

What do the circle colors mean?

Confidence in the assessment

High — converging lines of evidence, no meaningful dissent
Medium — evidence exists but is limited or indirect
Low — asserted in guidance, little or no evidence published
